## 粒子场 · Particle Fields

伸手搅动一团悬浮的粒子。八章轮流接管同一团粒子：旋度噪声、洛伦兹吸引子、霍普夫纤维化、叶序、克拉尼图形、密度波，然后是黑洞和极光。
Reach in and stir a floating cloud of particles. Eight chapters take turns driving it: curl noise, the Lorenz attractor, the Hopf fibration, phyllotaxis, Chladni figures, a density wave, then a black hole and an aurora.
→ [particle-fields/README.md](particle-fields/README.md)

## NEXUS / 2086 · 三境圆筒沙盘

工业港口、生态研究园区、废土配给站共用一个原地翻转的圆筒装置：货轮与机械臂、玻璃屋顶与双轨运输车、飞轮连杆与蒸汽动力。
An industrial harbour, an ecological research park and a wasteland ration station share one cylinder that flips in place.
→ [jelly-harbor/README.md](jelly-harbor/README.md) · [WASTELAND_DESIGN.md](jelly-harbor/WASTELAND_DESIGN.md)

## 入画 · Into the Painting

把凡·高、修拉、蒙克、透纳、莫奈、克里姆特、勃克林、弗里德里希的名画放进空间，九件作品各用一种算法，再配一个只属于它的交互。画作均为公有领域，图片来自 Wikimedia Commons。
Paintings by Van Gogh, Seurat, Munch, Turner, Monet, Klimt, Böcklin and Friedrich placed in space, each with an algorithm and an interaction of its own. All paintings are public domain; images from Wikimedia Commons.
→ [masterpieces/](masterpieces/)（离线烘焙脚本 offline bake scripts: `masterpieces/bake/`）

## 像素村落 · Pixel Village

太行山里的石头村，2.5D 像素风体素沙盘：28 个村民按职业和日程过日子，院门开合、天黑点灯，傍晚村口轮着唱戏、篝火、开村会。
A stone village in the Taihang Mountains as a 2.5D pixel-art voxel diorama, where 28 villagers live out their trades and routines.
→ [pixel-village/README.md](pixel-village/README.md)
