Bubble Machine泡泡制作机

Pump, silicone hose, brass nozzle, soap film · Interactive installation

One pump, and the hose swells into a bubble that travels down its length. At the brass nozzle it blows a soap bubble, breath by breath, until it lets go and drifts away.

Click / hold the pump — pump air (or press Space)
Press on the hose — pinch it, drag to squeeze forward
Click a bubble — pop it
In headset — pinch to click; press the handle or pop bubbles with your fingertip
